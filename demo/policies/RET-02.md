# Electronics and exceptions
Version: 1.0

## [RET-02.1] Electronics
Return window: 14 calendar days from purchase. The last day is included. Unopened electronics in their original packaging can be returned with proof of purchase.

## [RET-02.2] Opened or defective items
An opened electronic item requires review by a store lead. If the customer reports a defect, direct the request to the store lead for a warranty or defect assessment.

## [RET-02.3] Final-sale items
Items marked final sale are excluded from standard returns. A reported defect or damaged-on-arrival item should be reviewed by a store lead.
