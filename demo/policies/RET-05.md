# Pickup and delivery
Version: 1.0

## [RET-05.1] Ready-for-pickup holds
Online orders marked ready for pickup are held at the store for 7 calendar days. After that window, uncollected orders may be canceled and restocked. The customer should bring the order confirmation or ID matching the account name.

## [RET-05.2] Damaged on arrival
If an item arrives damaged or incomplete, do not process a standard return first. Document the damage, keep the packaging when possible, and escalate to the store lead for a carrier or fulfillment review.

## [RET-05.3] Missed or late delivery
When a customer reports a missed delivery window or a package marked delivered but not received, check the order status tool first. If the status does not resolve the issue, prepare an Operations handoff with the order number and reported delivery date.
