# Price match and promotions
Version: 1.0

## [RET-04.1] Price match
NovaMart may match a lower current price from an approved local competitor for the same in-stock item. The customer must show the competitor’s published price on the day of the request. Marketplace listings and flash sales are not eligible.

## [RET-04.2] Coupons and stacking
One store coupon or digital offer can be applied per transaction unless the offer text says otherwise. Member pricing and a single coupon may be combined when both are valid for the item. Expired coupons cannot be honored.

## [RET-04.3] Member discounts
Active NovaMart members receive the posted member price at checkout. Member discounts do not apply to final-sale items, gift cards, or services. If membership status cannot be verified, ask the store lead to review.
