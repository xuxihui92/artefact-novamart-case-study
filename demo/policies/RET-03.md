# Refunds and exchanges
Version: 1.0

## [RET-03.1] Refund method
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Allow 3–5 business days after the refund is processed for it to appear, depending on the payment provider.

## [RET-03.2] Exchanges
An exchange requires the original item to meet its applicable return policy. The replacement is subject to availability at the store.

## [RET-03.3] Exceptions and escalation
A store lead reviews missing proof of purchase, policy exceptions, and unavailable refund methods. A handoff should include the question, the applicable policy and the specific item or condition that needs review.
