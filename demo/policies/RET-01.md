# Standard returns
Version: 1.0

## [RET-01.1] Return window
Return window: 30 calendar days from purchase. The last day is included. This applies to general merchandise bought online or in a NovaMart store. The item must be unused and in resalable condition.

## [RET-01.2] Proof of purchase
Bring a receipt or an online order confirmation. A store associate can use the order number to check an eligible NovaMart purchase. If proof of purchase cannot be found, ask the store lead to review the request.

## [RET-01.3] Online purchases
Eligible items bought on NovaMart's website can be returned to a NovaMart store. Bring the item and the online order confirmation. Marketplace orders go through their original seller.
